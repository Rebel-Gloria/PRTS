# User Guide

PRTS uses your iPhone camera to find obstacles ahead. It can also describe the scene, answer questions, and read text aloud.

## Setup

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Action Button, choose Shortcut, and select Start or Stop PRTS.

## How to use

- **Start guidance**: Press the Action button.
- **Describe**: While guidance runs, press either volume button.
- **Ask**: Hold a volume button, speak after the tone, and release to send.
- **Read text**: Hold three fingers on the screen.
- **Stop guidance**: Press the Action button again.

You can also tap Start on the screen to start guidance, and hold the main button for 1 second to stop. To keep the volume buttons for volume, turn off Volume button shortcut in Intelligence settings and use the on-screen photo button instead.

## What you can set

- **Intelligence settings**: Choose what describes the scene and answers questions, and set up the photo button and text reading. By default it runs offline on your iPhone, and photos never leave it. You can switch to an online service.
- **Enhanced mode**: Available with the on-device provider and off by default. Allows broader voice answers but may reduce accuracy. It does not affect short descriptions or text reading.
- **Text reader**: Choose VLLM or Apple Vision. VLLM is the default and follows your provider in Intelligence settings. If the remote service is unavailable, it silently uses the on-device model.
- **Voice**: Turn announcements on or off, and set the speech rate and language.
- **Display**: Hide the camera view to save battery, or show the scan mesh.

## Important

- PRTS is an aid. It doesn't replace a cane or a guide dog.
- Guidance requires a Pro model with LiDAR. It can't detect steps or drop-offs yet.
- Guidance cues pause while describing, answering, or reading text.
- Descriptions and answers can be wrong. Rely on the guidance cues to decide whether you can walk ahead.

You can open this guide again anytime from the bottom of Settings.

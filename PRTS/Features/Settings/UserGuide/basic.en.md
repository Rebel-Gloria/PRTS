# User Guide

PRTS uses your iPhone camera to detect obstacles ahead, describe your surroundings, answer questions, read text aloud, and announce live movement.

## How to use

- **Start guidance**: Tap Start at the bottom of the screen, say "Start PRTS" to Siri, or assign Start PRTS in Settings > Accessibility > Touch > Back Tap.
- **Stop guidance**: Touch and hold the main button for 1 second. If Tap to stop is on, or if you use VoiceOver, tap or double-tap with two fingers instead.
- **Describe the scene**: While guidance runs, press either volume button—or tap the on-screen photo button—for a brief description of what's ahead.
- **Ask a question**: Press and hold a volume button or the photo button, speak after the tone, then release to send.
- **Live captions**: Press a volume button or the photo button three times in a row to hear ongoing actions, and press once to stop. Live captions require Allow cloud processing, a Qwen API key, and an internet connection.
- **Read text**: Hold three fingers on the screen and keep your iPhone steady to capture and hear text on signs, papers, or objects.

To keep the volume buttons for adjusting volume, turn off Volume button shortcut under Interaction in Settings and use the on-screen photo button instead.

## Settings

- **Intelligence**: Choose whether descriptions, questions, and text reading run on your device or through an online service. Turning off Allow cloud processing won’t stop an operation you’ve already started. When you choose On device for questions, your photos and questions stay on your device. Live captions always use Qwen.
- **Interaction**: Turn on Low vision mode (keeps the camera and smart features on while pausing depth sensing and guidance cues), Tap to stop, Volume button shortcut, Three-finger text reading, or No camera preview.
- **Language and Speech**: Turn voice announcements on or off, and adjust speech rate or language.

## Important

- PRTS is an aid and doesn't replace a white cane or a guide dog.
- Guidance requires a Pro model with LiDAR and can't detect steps or drop-offs yet.
- You’ll feel two taps when guidance starts. Silence doesn’t mean the way ahead is clear. If you stop and start again, or return to guidance after reading text, wait for a new cue.
- If your iPhone can’t see the ground, it won’t say the way is clear or tell you to turn. It can still say “Obstacle close” if a nearby obstacle fills the view.
- If your iPhone briefly loses sight of the way ahead, it can only use what it just detected. If you move beyond that area and it still can’t see ahead, it alerts you once.
- Guidance speech and vibration pause while PRTS describes a scene, answers a question, or shows Settings. With live captions, you can still feel guidance cues. While reading text, PRTS stops checking for obstacles.
- Descriptions and answers can be wrong. Use your cane along with guidance cues before moving ahead.

You can open this guide again anytime from the bottom of Settings.
